name: Serena inventory roles
description: Role visibility requirement for the inventory management area in Serena.
---

Inventory administration is for staff only. Normal users should not see the inventory-management entry or open its page; public availability questions can remain part of the customer support flow.

**Why:** The user clarified that the inventory button should be available to staff, not normal users.

**How to apply:** Keep the inventory link conditional on the staff role and guard the inventory route; distinguish the staff management page from customer-facing availability assistance.
